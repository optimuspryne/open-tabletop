Playing and creating

Mouse, keyboard, and touch

Common controls for the camera, pieces, cards, and selection.

Camera

Action Mouse Touch
Orbit Left-drag empty table space One-finger drag
Pan Right-drag empty table space Two-finger drag
Zoom Mouse wheel while nothing is held Two-finger pinch
Return to seat Seat > My Seat Same control
View from above Seat > Bird's Eye View Same control

Pieces

Action Mouse or keyboard Touch
Move Hold and drag a piece; release to drop Finger drag
Raise or lower a held piece Wheel, W/S, or up/down arrows Pinch while holding or use the hold controls
Rotate a held piece Alt-drag; A/D or left/right arrows Twist with a second finger or use rotate controls
Inspect Double-click Double-tap
Open actions Right-click a piece Long-press a piece
Stand upright or lay flat U, or piece menu Piece menu
Toggle grid snapping G, or piece menu Piece menu
Ping the table Middle-click empty space Long-press empty space

Ordinary cards use right-click to flip instead of opening the piece menu. Use ContextMenu / Shift+F10 for their other actions, or long-press on touch. Without a selection, the keyboard menu cycles through table pieces.

Cards and decks

Action Mouse Touch
Take a table card to hand Left-click Tap or Take to hand in the menu
Flip a table card Right-click Flip in the menu
Draw a card from a deck Left-drag from the deck Finger drag
Take the top card to hand Click the deck Tap the deck
Shuffle or split a deck Right-click, then choose the action Long-press, then choose the action
Move the whole deck Right-drag Press and drag the Move menu action
Privately inspect the top card Double-click Double-tap

In the inspection view, F places the card face-up, D places it face-down, H takes it to hand, and R returns it. Touch users can use the four labeled buttons.

Your hand

Drag or click a hand card out with the left mouse button to play it face-down. Use the right button to play it face-up. On touch, use one finger for face-down and two for face-up.

Turn on Rearrange to reorder your cards without playing them. In Rearrange mode, the Rank and Suit controls sort the hand. Use the hand's scroll controls when cards extend beyond the visible area.

Select several pieces

Use Shift-drag on empty table space to draw a selection box, or Shift-click to add and remove pieces. On touch, turn on Multi-Select, then drag a box or tap pieces.

Selection actions can stand pieces upright, change grid snapping, flip cards, roll dice, take cards to hand, rotate the group, or delete selected pieces. Press Escape or turn off Multi-Select to leave the mode.

More controls

With nothing held or selected, W/A/S/D or the arrow keys pan the camera. Seat > Lean In / Lean Out adjusts your view. Middle-click an unheld piece to highlight it for the table; touch users choose Highlight from its action menu.

GMs use L or Labels… to name a piece and set eligible low-stock warnings. Deck browsing, notecard editing, fog, and the whiteboard have their own focused controls; see Cards, dice, and pieces.

On narrow or touch layouts, close the expanded hand tray to bring back Multi-Select, Seat, and Table Actions. Full and compact labels are controlled in More > Settings > UI, or Menu > Table > Settings.

Keyboard access

Use Tab to move between interface controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. ContextMenu or Shift+F10 opens actions for the selected pieces. More > How to Play (or Menu > Table > How to Play) lists the controls available to your role.
