    How to Play

    Getting around

    • Left-drag the empty table to orbit the camera; right-drag to pan.
    • Scroll wheel to zoom (or raise/lower a piece you're holding).
    • Middle-mouse-click anywhere on the table to send a visual ping to other players.
    • Interactions → ↩ My Seat snaps the camera back to your seat.
    • Interactions → 🔎 Lean In zooms your view slightly. As if you were leaning over the table to get a closer look.

    Pieces & Objects & Dice

    • Left-drag a piece or die to pick it up, move, and throw it. Scroll to change its height; middle-click while holding snaps its facing to 90°.
    • Double-Left-click a piece, card, or die to inspect it up close. When inspecting, you can change the colors using the color picker(s), drag it to rotate, and click away to close.
    • After clicking a piece, press Delete to remove it, or U to stand it upright / lay it flat.

    Cards & Decks

    • Left-click a card on the table to take it into your hand; right-click it to flip it face-up.
    • Left-drag from a deck to draw the top card; left-click the deck to deal it face-down beside the deck.
    • Double-Left-click a deck to peek at the top card, then F (face-up), D (face-down), H (to hand), or R (return).
    • Right-click a deck to shuffle it; right-drag to move the whole deck.
    • Double-Right-click a deck to 'split' it.

    Your Hand

    • Your hand sits at the bottom of the screen — only you can see it.
    • Drag a card out onto the table: from a left-drag (or left-click) it lands face-down, from a right-drag (or right-click) face-up.
    • Click the ▲ on the right to hide your hand from your view. Click the '🃏 View Hand' button to bring it back.
    • Interactions → 🃏 Show Hand briefly reveals chosen cards to players you pick.

    Tools

    • 📝 Private Notes is a scratchpad only you see.
    • 📊 Scoreboard shows the shared scores. It's GM+Helper controlled.
    • ⏱️ Timer is a shared stopwatch / countdown.
    • 🖊️ Whiteboard — when the GM has it out, double-click the board to draw. Pen, eraser, and clear appear while you draw; Esc or Done hands it back.

    Interactions

    • 🔎 Lean In zooms your view slightly. As if you were leaning over the table to get a closer look.
    • ↩ My Seat snaps the camera back to your seat.
    • 🔄 Roll Dice rolls every die on the table.
    • 🃏 Show Hand briefly reveals chosen cards to players you pick.

    Helper abilities

    • Use the top toolbar to add 🎲 Dice, ♟️ 3D Objects, and 🎴 Decks — built-in or from the library.
    • In the Scoreboard you can add, rename, adjust, and clear score rows.

    GM controls

    • 🟫 Change Game Board swaps the board (built-in or library).
    • ⚙️ Room Controls — admit / kick / promote members, load a scene, resize the table, load a new skybox, toggle the whiteboard, and reset the table.
    • Room notes (inside the Scoreboard) are shared GM notes only you can edit.
    • 🖊️ Whiteboard — show or hide it, slide it around the table, and pick chalkboard or whiteboard.
    • Next turn passes the active turn to the next seat.
