        How to Play

        Getting around

        • Left-drag the empty table to orbit the camera; right-drag to pan.
        • Scroll wheel to zoom (or raise/lower a piece you're holding).
        • Tools → ↩ My Seat snaps the camera back to your seat.

        Pieces & objects

        • Left-drag a piece to pick it up, move, and throw it. Scroll to change its height; middle-click while holding snaps its facing to 90°.
        • Double-click a piece to inspect it up close — drag to rotate, click away to close.
        • After clicking a piece, press Delete to remove it, or U to stand it upright / lay it flat.

        Cards & decks

        • Left-click a card on the table to take it into your hand; right-click it to flip it face-up.
        • Left-drag from a deck to draw the top card; left-click the deck to deal it face-down beside the deck.
        • Double-click a deck to peek at the top card, then F (face-up), D (face-down), H (to hand), or R (return).
        • Right-click a deck to shuffle it; right-drag to move the whole deck.

        Your hand

        • Your hand sits at the bottom of the screen — only you can see it.
        • Drag a card out onto the table: from a left-drag it lands face-down, from a right-drag face-up.
        • Tools → 🃏 Show Hand briefly reveals chosen cards to players you pick.

        Tools

        • 🔄 Roll Dice rolls every die on the table. 📝 Private Notes is a scratchpad only you see.
        • 📊 Scoreboard shows the shared scores; ⏱️ Timer is a shared stopwatch / countdown.
        • 🖊️ Whiteboard — when the GM has it out, double-click the board to draw. Pen, eraser, and clear appear while you draw; Esc or Done hands it back.

        Helper abilities

        • Use the top toolbar to add 🎲 Dice, ♟️ 3D Objects, and 🎴 Decks — built-in or from the library.
        • In the Scoreboard you can add, rename, adjust, and clear score rows.

        GM controls

        • 🟫 Change Game Board swaps the board (built-in or library).
        • ⚙️ Room Controls — admit / kick / promote members, load a scene, resize the table, toggle the whiteboard, and reset the table.
        • Room notes (inside the Scoreboard) are shared GM notes only you can edit.
        • 🖊️ Whiteboard — show or hide it, slide it around the table, and pick chalkboard or whiteboard.
        • Next turn passes the active turn to the next seat.
