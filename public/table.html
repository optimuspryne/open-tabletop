        How to Play

        -- Getting Around --

        • Left-click-hold + drag in an empty space to rotate your view around; right-click-hold + drag to pan up, down, left, right.
        • Use the mouse scroll wheel to zoom in and out (or raise/lower a piece you're holding).
        • Scroll wheel click anywhere on the table to send a visual ping to all players.
        • Interactions → ↩ My Seat snaps the camera back to your designated seat.
        • Interactions → 🔎 Lean In zooms your view in slightly, to simulate leaning over the table. Interactions → 🔎 Lean Out to return.

        -- Pieces & Objects --

        • Left-click-hold a piece to pick it up, move, and let go of left-click to drop or throw it. Scroll to change its height; Middle-click while holding rotates its facing in 45° steps.
        • Double-left-click a piece to inspect it up close. From here you can change its colors, drag to rotate, or click away to close the view.
        • After left-clicking a piece, press Delete to remove it, U to stand it upright / lay it flat, or G to toggle snap-to-grid (when the table has a grid).

        -- Cards & decks --

        • Left-click a card on the table to take it into your hand; right-click it to flip it face-up.
        • Left-click-hold + drag from a deck to draw the top card; left-click the deck to deal it face-down beside the deck.
        • Double-click a deck to peek at the top card, then F (face-up), D (face-down), H (to hand), or R (return).
        • Right-click a deck to shuffle it; Right-click-hold to pick up and move the whole deck.
        • Double-right-click a deck to split it.

        -- Your Private Hand --

        • Your private hand sits at the bottom of the screen, and only you can see it. Other players will see your hand fanned-out face-down at your seat.
        • Drag a card out onto the table: from a left-click-drag (or a single left-click) it lands face-down, from a right-click-drag (or a single right-click) it lands face-up.
        • Click the '▾' button to hide your hand from your view. Click the '🃏 Show hand (1)' button to bring it back into view.
        • Interactions → 🃏 Show Hand briefly reveals chosen cards to players you pick.
        • Interactions → 🂠 Drop Hand drop your entire hand on the table either face-up or face-down.

        -- Tools --

        • 📝 Private Notes is a scratchpad only you see.
        • 💬 Chat is a public chat. If there are messages you have missed, the icon visually indicate so.
        • 📊 Scoreboard shows the shared scores.
        • ⏱️ Timer is a shared stopwatch / countdown.
        • 🖊️ Whiteboard — when the GM has it out, double-click the board to draw. Pen, eraser, and clear appear while you draw; Esc or Done hands it back.

        -- Interactions --

        • 🔎 Lean In zooms your view in slightly, to simulate leaning over the table. Press 🔎 Lean Out to return.
        • ↩ My Seat snaps the camera back to your designated seat.
        • 🔄 Roll Dice rolls all dice currently on the table.
        • 🃏 Show Hand briefly reveals chosen cards in your hand to players you pick.
        • 🂠 Drop Hand drop your entire hand on the table either face-up or face-down.

        -- Helper Abilities --

        • 🎲 Dice to quickly spawn dice.
        • 🧱 Browse Built-Ins add built-in decks and objects to the table.
        • 📚 Browse Custom Library add custom decks and objects to the table.
        • Tools → 📊 Scoreboard add, rename, adjust, and clear score rows.

        -- GM Abilities --

        • 🎲 Dice to quickly spawn dice.
        • 🧱 Browse Built-Ins add built-in decks, boards, objects and skyboxes to the table.
        • 📚 Browse Custom Library add custom decks, boards, objects and skyboxes to the table.
        • Tools → 📊 Scoreboard add, rename, adjust, and clear score rows. You can also edit the 'Room Notes' section.
        • Tools → 🖊️ Whiteboard show the whiteboard, set its style, and specify its location in real-time.
        • Next Turn button proceeds turn-order to the next person.

        -- Room Controls --

        • 👥 Members List lets you approve entries, promote players to helpers or GMs, and also kick players permanently from the room.
        • 📽️ Load a Scene lets you load a published scene. This could be a ready-to-chess game, etc.
        • 💾 Save Table State saves the table in its current configuration. As a saftey precaution, the table is automatically saved once the last GM has left the room.
        • 🎨 Customize Table lets to change the dimensions of the play area in real-time, and set a custom color for the table felt.
        • 🌄 Skybox to browse all public skyboxes. Hit 'apply' on any to change the current skybox.
        • 🧹 Reset to clear the table of all objects, boards, decks and also everyone's private hands.
