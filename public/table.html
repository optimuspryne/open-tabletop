ControlsHide ▾
Left-Click
  • Take any card on the field and put it in your hand.
  • Take top card of a deck and place it face-down next to the deck.
  • Place card from your hand face-down in a pre-determined location on the field.
  • After clicking an object, press Delete to remove it.
  • With an object held or hovered, press U to toggle stand-upright / lie-flat (on by default for chess, go, checkers, tokens & decks).
Double Left-Click
  • Inspect objects/cards on the field.
    • Click and drag to rotate them.
  • Inspect the top card of a deck.
    • Press F to place it next to the deck face-up.
    • Press D to place it next to the deck face-down.
    • Press H to place it in your hand.
    • Press R (or click outside the preview) to return it to the top of the deck.
Left-Click + Drag
  • Rotate your view of the board.
  • Pick up / move / throw objects & cards. Scroll the wheel to change height.
  • While holding, Middle-Click to snap facing to the nearest 90°.
  • Draw the top card from a deck and move/throw it.
  • Drag a card out of your hand onto the field, face-down.
Right-Click
  • Shuffle any deck.
  • After clicking a deck, press S to save it.
  • Reveal the face of any card on the field.
  • Place a card from your hand onto the field, face-up.
Right-Click + Drag
  • Pan your view of the board.
  • Hold and move any deck.
  • Drag a card out of your hand onto the field, face-up.
